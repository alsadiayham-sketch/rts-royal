# RTS

## Register

brand

## Users

Arabic-speaking business owners evaluating a website, desktop or mobile application,
or POS system. English-speaking visitors can switch the full interface to English.
Owners browse on phones between customers and need readable, concrete service information.

## Product Purpose

Present RTS - Royal Technology Solutions as one partner for websites, desktop software,
iOS and Android applications, and point of sale across retail and hospitality.
Convert interest into an explicit WhatsApp conversation, not an automatic message or payment.
The confirmed initial business number is managed through authenticated site settings.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, approachable. Retain the supplied angular R mark and royal purple
but create a distinct identity, not a recoloring of the ADA reference.

## Anti-references

Avoid the reference site's decorative glass cards, excessive gradients, generic claims,
invented customers or reviews, and performance metrics without evidence.
Never present an illustrative POS interface as a live customer system.

## Design Principles

- Show the business outcome and a concrete example together.
- Distinguish custom software services from the existing POS product.
- Make the entire Arabic and English experience equally usable.
- Present RTS POS as the production Windows point-of-sale product.
- Keep administration private and separate from public marketing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, keyboard-operable navigation, reduced-motion support,
semantic HTML, usable mobile layouts, and Arabic RTL with correct LTR numbers.
